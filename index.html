Flight View

How far the onboard camera points above level. Adjust in flight with ↑ and ↓. More uptilt suits faster flying, since the airframe leans further forward to get there.

How far the picture trails the drone. Analog runs about 20–30 ms glass to glass, digital 30–40; past 100 ms is a failing link. Only the view lags – the sticks and the physics stay live, so you hit things that were already there.

You stand a few metres behind the spawn point and the view tracks the drone automatically. Lower FOV zooms in for distant flying.
